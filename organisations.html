TAPMI Executive MBA: Sponsored Leadership Pathway

Develop high-potential managers through a comprehensive part-time degree experience. Combines the rigorous academic core with an enterprise sponsor layer, relationship support, and a workplace capstone addressing an internal strategic initiative.

Operational Accelerator

TAPMI Execution Leadership Accelerator (MDP)

A 6-week customised cohort intervention addressing a specific organisational execution bottleneck. Begins with a capability diagnostic, works on a live business problem, and concludes with an Execution Action Plan and 30-day impact review.

  • ✓ Audience: Middle managers & emerging business-unit leaders (min 20 participants)
  • ✓ Format: 6 weeks blended with 4 intensive workshops & action coaching
  • ✓ Sponsor Value: Direct diagnostic, live business challenge & 30-day review
  • ✓ Proposed Fee: Rs 70,000 per participant plus GST (20 seats = Rs 14,00,000 before GST)

Sponsor Integration & Governance

Proposed relationship-management protocols ensure organizational investment translates into validated management capability while respecting learner consent:

Manager Briefing

Structured alignment between the candidate, nominating sponsor, and academic mentor before cohort kickoff.

Milestone Updates

Agreed attendance and term-end progress notes shared with sponsoring HR/L&D with candidate consent.

Capstone Alignment

Confidential workplace problem selected in consultation with the sponsor to produce verifiable internal ROI.
