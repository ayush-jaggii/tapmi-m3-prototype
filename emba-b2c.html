Proposed B2C Individual Pathway

Lead beyond your function without putting your career on hold

TAPMI Executive MBA: From Functional Expert to Business Leader

A proposed part-time blended Executive MBA for experienced professionals who want broader business understanding, cross-functional confidence and a workplace project they can apply to a real management challenge.

Academic Evaluation Notice: Programme designs, fees and services shown here are student proposals for academic evaluation. They are not approved TAPMI offers or an invitation to enrol.
Target Audience

Mid-Career Specialists

Typically ~5–10 years into careers across technology, product, BFSI, and corporate services seeking transition to general management. (Targeting description, not formal admission eligibility).

Practical Value

Career Continuity

Continue working full-time; connect classroom concepts immediately to your job; collaborate across functional domains; and execute an applied workplace capstone.

Proposed Delivery

Blended Format

Live interactive sessions, digital support modules, real-world case discussions, simulations, and peer learning. No invented duration or fixed intake rules.

Global Dimension

Study Abroad Without Stepping Away

A faculty-led international immersion module is built into your EMBA journey, so you gain global exposure while working full-time.

Proposed Individual Learning Journey

Structured to build holistic enterprise perspective from functional foundations to executive synthesis:

Step 1

Orientation & Goals

Personal leadership self-assessment, cohort onboarding, and individual development planning.

Step 2

Business Foundations

Enterprise strategy, financial accounting, marketing dynamics, supply chain, and data analytics.

Step 3

People Leadership

Navigating organizational politics, team delegation, coaching, and talent management.

Step 4

Cross-Functional Decisions

Commercial viability, technical tradeoffs, capital budgeting, and ethical governance.

Step 5

Workplace Application

Identifying and scoping a live business challenge in your current operational domain.

Step 6

Capstone Review

Final defense of your intervention plan and roadmap before an academic and practitioner panel.

Individual Pathway FAQs

Is the programme conducted entirely online?

The proposal outlines a blended delivery combining scheduled interactive live discussions, digital asynchronous study, and applied workplace exercises. Exact campus contact requirements remain unapproved.

What does the workplace capstone involve?

Participants identify a real business challenge within their current organization or industry domain, formulating a rigorous intervention with confidentiality protections and academic faculty guidance.

Who is eligible to apply?

The academic concept targets experienced mid-career professionals (typically ~5–10 years). Formal admission eligibility, degree recognition, and prerequisites would be established institutionally.

Are salary hikes or job promotions guaranteed?

No. Professional education builds capability and confidence, but career progression depends on individual performance, organizational context, and market conditions.

Can I enrol or submit an official application here?

No. This website operates strictly as an academic prototype for Group 4's coursework. No official applications or admissions transactions are processed.
