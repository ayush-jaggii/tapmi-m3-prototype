Proposed B2B Customised MDP

Turn a management challenge into an execution plan

TAPMI Execution Leadership Accelerator

A proposed customised Management Development Programme for middle managers and emerging business-unit leaders. The design begins with your organisation's capability gap and uses a live business problem throughout the learning journey.

Academic Evaluation Notice: Programme designs, fees and services shown here are student proposals for academic evaluation. They are not approved TAPMI offers or an invitation to enrol.
Target Audience

Middle Management

L&D and HR heads with business sponsors in GCCs, IT/services, manufacturing, BFSI, and logistics seeking structured team capability acceleration.

Proposed Format

6-Week Blended

Dedicated cohorts of 20–30 participants; 4 intensive workshops (on campus or client site); digital resources, simulations, coaching, and a 30-day impact review.

Tangible Output

Execution Action Plan

Teams formulate and defend a concrete implementation plan tackling an actual operational bottleneck with agreed success metrics.

Global Dimension

Optional Global Track

Extend any customised MDP with an international immersion track: company visits and cross-border case work, co-designed with your L&D team around your industry's markets.

The 6-Week Learning Journey

An 8-stage intervention structured around diagnostic scoping, execution frameworks, and post-programme accountability:

Stage 1

Diagnose

Capability diagnostic, sponsor scoping discussion, and agreeing target KPIs.

Stage 2

From Strategy to Execution

Prioritisation, cascading accountability, and decision rights.

Stage 3

Leading Through People

Delegation, high-stakes feedback, and performance coaching.

Stage 4

Cross-Functional Execution

Stakeholder alignment, cross-silo influence, and process coordination.

Stage 5

Execution Under Uncertainty

Adaptive scenario planning and data-supported managerial decisions.

Stage 6

Action-Learning Project

Teams dissect and map the sponsor's live organisational problem.

Stage 7

Executive Review

Diagnosis, proposed intervention, implementation roadmap, and KPIs presented to sponsors.

Stage 8

30-Day Impact Review

Post-programme implementation tracking against agreed business indicators.

Explicit Academic Illustration

Illustrative Project Scenario

Illustrative project, not a reported client result: “A cross-functional team maps delays in the sales-to-operations handoff, identifies ownership gaps between commercial and fulfillment teams, and proposes a revised handoff protocol with agreed service-level review measures.”

This example illustrates how participants apply workshop tools to live friction. It does not represent an actual past institutional client or guaranteed percentage improvement.

MDP Accelerator FAQs

For whom is this accelerator intended?

It is designed for middle managers, emerging business-unit leaders, and team leads nominated by client organisations facing execution bottlenecks.

Can individual professionals enrol on an open basis?

No. The Execution Leadership Accelerator is an organisational cohort intervention requiring a minimum cohort of 20 participants sponsored by an employer.

How is the curriculum customised?

Customisation starts during Stage 1 with a diagnostic scoping call. Case examples, role-plays, and the capstone project are drawn directly from the sponsoring client's industry and internal bottlenecks.

Does this guarantee business results or ROI?

No. While the design includes agreed review metrics and a 30-day post-programme impact review, academic proposals do not guarantee external financial or business results.

Is this currently available to book?

No. This is an academic concept developed for course evaluation, not an official TAPMI admissions or corporate training offering.
