Build your next generation of leaders while they keep delivering

TAPMI Executive MBA: Sponsored Leadership Pathway

A proposed employer-sponsored Executive MBA pathway for high-potential managers. The academic core is shared with self-funded learners, with a sponsor layer that connects development to a live organisational challenge.

Academic Evaluation Notice: Programme designs, fees and services shown here are student proposals for academic evaluation. They are not approved TAPMI offers or an invitation to enrol.
Target Audience

Corporate Talent Bench

HR/L&D heads, business-unit heads and corporate sponsors in technology, GCCs, services and industrial organisations looking to develop and retain high-potential talent.

Practical Value

Cross-Functional Acumen

Broader cross-functional capability; learning while working; sponsor-aligned workplace capstone; and clear sponsor communication with learner consent.

Proposed Format

Part-Time Blended

Scheduled live discussion, digital support, cases, simulations, peer learning and workplace application. Exact duration and calendar require institutional approval.

Proposed 5-Phase Learning Journey

A structured pathway that anchors foundational business knowledge directly in the sponsor's operational context:

Phase 1

Nomination & Sponsor Briefing

Candidate selection, initial sponsor alignment call, and baseline capability scoping.

Phase 2

Business Foundations

Core strategy, corporate finance, marketing management, operations, and business analytics.

Phase 3

People Leadership

Leading high-performing teams, delegation, coaching conversations, and change management.

Phase 4

Cross-Functional Decisions

Negotiation, enterprise resource allocation, stakeholder influence, and ethical governance.

Phase 5

Workplace Capstone & Review

Live organisational problem, diagnosis, intervention plan, implementation roadmap, and success measures.

Enterprise Value

The Proposed Sponsor Layer

To ensure maximum return on organizational sponsorship, the programme incorporates structured relationship management protocols:

Relationship Support

Dedicated relationship management liaison to coordinate cohort logistics, executive briefing sessions, and academic scheduling.

Term-End Progress Notes

Agreed milestone and attendance summaries shared with sponsoring HR/L&D (subject to explicit candidate consent and confidentiality agreements).

Capstone Alignment

Capstone projects are aligned directly with the sponsor's strategic initiatives, delivering measurable actionable recommendations.

Global Immersion Week

Your sponsored cohort steps into one international immersion week: live company diagnostics and executive peer exchange in a global business hub. Designed so participants stay productive before and after the module.

Governance Note: Academic standards and grading remain the sole prerogative of the academic institution. Employment terms and bonding arrangements remain employer/employee internal matters.

Organisations may agree to a 3-year nomination framework: agreed annual nominations, annual fee escalation capped at 5% after year one, and unused seats roll over for one subsequent cohort. Self-funded founding-cohort concessions do not apply or stack. Precise terms require bilateral agreement.

Sponsored Pathway FAQs

Is this a different academic programme from the individual EMBA?

No. It shares the same proposed academic core, faculty rigor, and standards as the individual Executive MBA. The key distinction is the enterprise sponsor layer connecting development directly to an organisational challenge.

Can employees continue working while enrolled?

Yes. The proposed part-time blended format supports career continuity, combining scheduled sessions with digital support so participants maintain ongoing business delivery.

What progress details does the sponsor receive?

Agreed attendance and milestone reporting is shared, provided learner consent is obtained and appropriate corporate confidentiality safeguards are maintained.

Is enrolment currently open?

No. This is an academic proposal developed by Group 4 for course evaluation. It is not an official TAPMI admissions service, and applications are not being accepted.
