TAPMI Certificate in AI for Business Applications

A proposed six-week certificate for business students, recent graduates and early-career professionals, combining practical assignments, live learning and an applied portfolio.

Academic Evaluation Notice: Programme designs, fees and services shown here are student proposals for academic evaluation. They are not approved TAPMI offers or an invitation to enrol.
Audience

Early-Career & Students

Business students, recent graduates, and early-career professionals wanting practical, responsible AI skills for marketing, analytics, and operational tasks.

Workload

4–6 Hours Weekly

2 hours self-paced preparation, 1.5 hours Saturday live masterclass, and 1.5–2.5 hours applied portfolio work. 6 weeks total duration.

Output & Assessment

Managerial AI Audit

Portfolio deliverable evaluated on a 30% exercise / 70% capstone rubric with a 70% minimum passing score. Institutional certificate/badge requires approval.

6-Week Curriculum Structure

Week 1

AI Fundamentals & Responsible Use

Core concepts, enterprise risks, data privacy, and ethical frameworks.

Week 2

Prompting & Managerial Productivity

Workflow automation, document synthesis, and multi-turn prompt engineering.

Week 3

Marketing & Customer Insights

Audience segmentation, campaign drafting, and synthetic customer personas.

Week 4

Analysis & Decision Support

Spreadsheet analysis, scenario modeling, and automated executive summarisation.

Week 5

Finance, HR & Operations

Domain-specific workflows, audit checks, and process bottleneck identification.

Week 6

Applied Project & Portfolio Defense

Final Managerial AI Audit Portfolio submission and peer presentation.
